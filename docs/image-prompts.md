## Style for every prompt
- Real photographs with a world-class photographer's eye, shot candidly the way the best music-video, album and youth-culture photographers shoot a real night with friends: decisive moments caught, never staged; one clear subject; real light (window light, warm lamps, a paper lantern, streetlight, dawn) or a direct on-camera flash for some night frames; phone or compact-camera optics (wide, deep focus); real exposure (blown windows, deep shadows, mixed warm and cool light, motion blur in low light); imperfect crops; real details in nice, lived-in places; real skin texture and flyaway hair; everyday tasteful clothes.
- Each prompt starts by saying what kind of photo it is ("Candid phone photo, ...", "Snapshot with on-camera flash, ...") and ends with "No text, no captions, no watermark."
- Never use: editorial, cinematic, fashion photographer, beautifully lit, film grain, 8k, masterpiece, hyperrealistic, award-winning, bokeh. Avoid the AI tells: symmetrical posed groups, everyone smiling at the lens, rim-light glow, HDR sheen, flawless skin, model looks, matching outfits, complicated hands.
- Phones show only a plain glow or a plain back: no text, logos or real app UI. Stylish, never sexualized; everyone is a young adult (19–22).
- The cast recurs all night (from C1): Jess (Black, short natural coils, small gold hoops, cream shirt), Kai (East Asian, black curtain fringe, white T-shirt under an open black overshirt), Dev (South Asian, thick wavy black hair, short beard, rust overshirt) and Sam (white, big curly auburn hair, freckles, light denim jacket), with Maya (19, European, long tousled chestnut-to-caramel hair, light grey-green eyes, straight dark brows, black cropped jacket over a black top, thin silver chain, silver rings). Every prompt restates the outfits. Every group shot includes at least one Black, one East Asian and one South Asian young adult.
- Print prints every P image in two inks on cream newsprint, so the frames are built on value: big light grounds (a lamp-washed wall or ceiling, a bright doorway, the midsummer sky) against dark shapes. The night is Saturday 14 June, so night skies stay a glowing blue, not black.
