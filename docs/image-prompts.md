# 0% image prompts (Flatline)

18 stills (R12–R14 added on 2026-10-13 for the three Maya moments in the second half). There is no longer an image cap (Tom, 2026-10-13); for the Print treatment, new stills are printed in two inks, so they don't need the glossy editorial style below. Six are for the screen world (before the 1:24 breakout) and nine for the real world (after it). R1 and R2 were cut on 2026-10-13 (too much like a messy house party); drop 1 now opens on R8. The other feed posts in the phone UI are motion graphics, so they need no images.

## How to use these
- **Maya (paste into every prompt that shows her):** "Maya, a 19-year-old young woman of European appearance, slim, with high cheekbones, large almond-shaped light grey-green eyes, straight dark brows, a straight slim nose, long layered tousled hair, dark chestnut brown melting into caramel ends, modern dark club clothing (black cropped jacket, silver jewellery), a calm, intense, confident look." Keep her from behind, in profile, or soft and out of focus where the shot allows, so she stays consistent across shots. Stylish, never sexualized.
- **Consistency:** generate `M0` first and use it as the reference image for every other shot with Maya in it. In Runway's text-to-image model (gen4_image) that means passing it as a reference image tagged `maya` and writing `@maya` in the prompt in place of the description.
- **Size:** 16:9 (1920×1080). Keep the subject in the middle third so a 9:16 crop also works for the vertical edit.
- **No text, logos or real apps in any image.** Every phone screen in the stills shows only abstract glow; the TokTic UI is added in motion graphics.
- **Negative prompt (where the tool supports one):** text, watermark, logo, brand names, Instagram, TikTok, Apple logo, extra fingers, distorted hands, sexualized, revealing clothing, red plastic cups, mess, trashy.
- **Motion in the edit:** the stills are not shown static. Each gets a slow Ken Burns push or drift, and some get a parallax or light-leak pass, cut to the beat.
- File names: save each file as its ID plus a short name (e.g. `S1_face_blue.png`) in the Drive folder, so the build picks it up by ID.

## Style anchors
- **Screen world:** cinematic, cold blue phone-screen light, shallow depth of field, 35mm.
- **Real world (revised 2026-10-13):** editorial music-video still shot by a top fashion photographer, cinematic and beautifully lit, warm practical lights and soft haze, rich but natural colour, elegant styling, light and joyful, fine film grain, shallow depth of field, 50mm. Never a trashy party: no red cups, no mess, no harsh point-and-shoot flash.
- **Setting:** a stylish industrial loft at night with warm Edison bulbs, tall windows and a polished concrete floor; later a rooftop at dawn.
- **Cast:** every shot with a group includes at least one Black, one East Asian and one South Asian (Indian) young adult among a diverse, attractive cast of 19 to 22 year olds in curated going-out clothes.

## TokTic app colours (proposal)
TokTic is the fictional app on Maya's phone, a deliberate jab at the real one. Its palette stays clear of TikTok's own cyan, red and black, so it reads as parody, not imitation: a cool, slick UI that the warm real-world photos break through.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| bg | `#0B0D12` | app background |
| surface | `#151922` | cards, notification banners |
| text | `#F2F4F8` | primary text |
| muted | `#7A8194` | timestamps, secondary text |
| signal | `#C6FF3D` | TokTic accent (acid lime): logo, buttons, the scroll tunnel |
| pulse | `#FF4D6D` | likes, low battery, the 0% crack |
| charge | `#3DFF8A` | battery while above 20% |

The TokTic logo is the word "TokTic" set in a heavy rounded type with the dot of the i as a ticking clock hand, a nod to how the app eats time.
