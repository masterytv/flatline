# Shot list: story, places, cut plan and the new photo set

Proposed on 2026-10-13 for Tom's approval. Built from a code map of every section, three independent drafts (a music-video director, a motion designer and a photographer) and a judged merge. The prompts are in docs/image-prompts.md.

## Story

ONE NIGHT, ONE CAMERA ROLL (spine: the motion-designer draft). Saturday 14 June, a midsummer night, so the sky never goes fully black. Maya, 19, is at Jess's top-floor flat with her four friends: Jess (Black), Kai (East Asian), Dev (South Asian) and Sam (white, auburn curls). These are the names already in the opening UI and feed. She is there but not present, because she is the one behind the phone. Her lock screen is a photo she took of them. She films the party, posts herself, and scrolls the posts they made from the same room. She declines Jess's call. When Kai sends a photo of her hunched over the screen ('maya look up'), she turns on Do Not Disturb. At 'Clear!' the battery dies and she sees her own face in the black glass. The room pans back into view and the friends crowd the lens until she looks up, lit warm for the first time.
The phone shatters into the TokTic parts and the night becomes paper. She dances first, alone, while the friends sit on the sofa in their phones. Jess notices. One by one the stencils warm, and from the same viewpoint the sofa is empty and everyone is dancing. They take it up to the roof ('no pain, no fear, just light'). In the kitchen Sam puts Maya's phone on charge, and it fights back. It wakes on the beeps, the app's cold ink spreads over warm prints of the night, the notifications call her by name, a streak counts down, and 'Stay connected?' returns. On 'I came back to life' she holds it, lit cold, while Jess calls her from the doorway. On the drop-3 downbeat she flips it face-down, and the friends stack their phones on top of hers (the phone-stack game, from the director draft). Then a battery of real life charges one cell per beat as they run out: the lobby door, the corner shop, the middle of the empty street. The walk back becomes the zine's before and after and a wall of fly-posters. The final drop is back in the living room. On 'Dancing' the night's camera roll spills out and she is in every photo, circled in grease pencil: '3:17 am, everyone'. Sunrise on the roof, the walk home. In the morning the stack of phones is still face-down on the counter, 'BATTERY 100% · SCREEN TIME 0 MIN', and all five are asleep in a heap on the sofa where it started (the photographer draft's bookend).
WHAT CHANGED AND WHY:
(1) A recurring cast from one reference (C1), restated outfits in every prompt, and no random strangers in hero shots. Real camera rolls repeat faces, and the friends need to be known before the payoffs land.
(2) Real nice places, one per section: flat, roof, kitchen, street and corner shop, the walk back, the flat, dawn.
(3) Maya goes from behind every camera to inside every photo.
(4) The receipt becomes a threat aimed at her (Tom's fix).
(5) The pop-up book becomes a five-photo burst from the couch's own viewpoint (Tom's fix).
(6) DANCING after the burst is filled with new prints every 2 beats (Tom's fix).
(7) The 3:17 keepsake lands below a DANCING word that keeps re-stamping, so D...G is never covered (Tom's fix).
(8) Drop 3 opens on a decisive act: the flip, then the stack.
(9) Midsummer skies keep every night print readable.
The Print treatment, the three grafts and the opening's motion graphics are unchanged; only its photos and two small beats change (Kai's photo bubble, the post carousel).

## Places, in song order

1. 0:00-1:24.8: Jess's flat, the living room at 1:47 a.m., seen only through Maya's phone in colour. The lock screen is the friends on the front steps at dusk. The viewfinder, her post, Kai's photo of her and half the feed all show this room; three feed posts are other people's nights elsewhere (festival, jetty, gig). From 1:08 the same room is seen blurred through the dead glass: 5 pans from far to near, then the friends' faces come right up to the lens (1:17), then her warm face (1:21).

4. 1:54.3 drop 2: the sofa (stencils). 2:01.6: the floor in front of the same sofa (they join her).
5. 2:07.1 groove downbeat, the first outdoor frame: the roof terrace as they burst out of the stairwell door under a blue midsummer sky. The lantern is 'just light', a spin is 'forever', and the line against the stairwell wall is 'we move together'.
6. 2:22.7 (glimpsed through the scattering strips) and 2:23.6 beep 1: the kitchen. Her phone is on the charger; Jess calls from the doorway. 2:38.2: the flip and the phone stack on the counter.
7. 2:40.0 battery A, out of the lobby door. 2:45.5 battery B, the corner shop's glowing window. 2:47.3 battery C, the middle of the empty street.

9. 3:07.4 final drop: back in the living room for the peak. On 'Dancing' (3:16.46) the camera roll recaps the roof, the kitchen and the shop; the keepsake is taken at 3:17.
10. 3:21.9 the roof at sunrise; 3:24.0 the street at dawn.
11. 3:25.8 morning: the phone stack still face-down on the counter, then all five asleep on the same sofa.
12. 3:29: bare paper and the living line.
